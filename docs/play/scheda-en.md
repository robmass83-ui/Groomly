## Title

Groomly: grooming diary

## Short description

Diary, pet cards and price list for a grooming salon. Free.

## Long description

Groomly is the diary for a grooming salon. Keep the dogs, the cats and the appointments in one place, on the phone. The app is free.

Pet cards
Each animal has a name, breed, size, contacts, notes and photos. Prices written on the card stay as the salon saved them and, when you use them, they come before the price list.

Diary
See the day and the week. Add an appointment, choose the services and the duration, and close the visit when it is done. You can mark a personal commitment or a day when the salon is closed.

Regulars
Regular clients show up when it is time to book them again.

Price list
The price list by breed shows prices and times. Print it or share it as a PDF or an image, and you can also keep a price file you already have.

Earnings
At the end of the day you see what was taken. The report exports for the accountant, as a PDF or as Excel.

Salon data
Backup on the phone and, if you connect the account, a copy on Google Drive. An archive for animals no longer in care. Reminders for regulars, for visits still to close, and for a new version of the app.

Language
The interface follows the person’s language: Italian, English or Spanish. The price sheet for customers can stay in the salon’s language.

Account
You sign in with email. Client data stays with the salon.
